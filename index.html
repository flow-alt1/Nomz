For nine wonderful years, Nomz Truck served our community with delicious food, warm hospitality, and a passion for bringing people together.

Two years ago, when our beloved food truck broke down, we faced an unexpected turning point. But our love for cooking never stopped. Instead, we saw an opportunity to begin a new chapter.

Today, we're proud to introduce Nomz Kitchen, where we're bringing our passion back to its roots: authentic Persian home cooking.

Inspired by traditional family recipes, our meals are prepared with fresh ingredients, aromatic spices, and the care and attention that make homemade food so special. From comforting Persian stews to fragrant saffron rice and classic favorites, every dish is made to bring you the warmth, flavor, and feeling of a home-cooked meal.

We're grateful to everyone who supported Nomz Truck over the years, and we're excited to welcome both familiar faces and new friends to Nomz Kitchen.

A new chapter. The same passion. A taste of home.
